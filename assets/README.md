AO is multiplied into the color at load; normal and roughness maps feed the lit
shader. Impact, hit, headshot, kill, dry-fire, hurt, landing and bullet-crack sounds
are synthesized at startup in `src/audio.cpp` (no files), as are the brass cases'
tinks. Sounds are trimmed of leading (and, for the footstep loops, trailing) silence
and level-matched at load (see `src/audio.cpp`); the files themselves are unedited.
The reload recording is cut at load into its separate sounds (latch, cylinder out,
ejection, rounds in, spin, cylinder shut), each played on its moment in the reload
animation. The L42A1's recording (a Kar98k, the same kind of bolt action) is cut the
same way: the shot with its echo, the bolt opened, closed after a shot, closed on a fresh
magazine, and the magazine pushed home (steel scraping in the well spliced onto the click
that seats it).

The map's building textures are generated too (Blender's bundled numpy):

    blender --background --factory-startup --python tools/make_textures.py -- assets/textures

The revolver is built procedurally in Blender: a six-shot, double-action, swing-out
revolver made of separate moving parts (frame, grip, cylinder, crane, ejector rod and
star, hammer, trigger, latch) plus cartridges, fired cases and a speedloader, with
generated steel and checkered-walnut textures. `revolver.txt` lists the meshes and the
pivots the game animates them about. To regenerate (Blender 4.x):

    blender --background --factory-startup --python tools/make_revolver.py -- assets/models/revolver

The FAL is built the same way (it shares helpers in `tools/procgen.py`, and uses the
revolver's neutral steel textures):

    blender --background --factory-startup --python tools/make_fal.py -- assets/models/fal

and the L42A1 (its wood uses the FAL's walnut texture):

    blender --background --factory-startup --python tools/make_l42.py -- assets/models/l42

The guns' textures are shared (the models are unchanged; the steel and wood images that
`make_revolver.py` and `make_fal.py` still write next to their models are no longer used):

    blender --background --factory-startup --python tools/make_gun_textures.py -- assets/models/gun_textures

The character came without animations; `tools/export_character.py` authors them
(idle, walk, run, crouch, crouch_walk, rest, and the first-person fp_hold, which includes
the revolver's reload: the right hand carries the gun through it and the left hand works
the latch, ejector, speedloader and cylinder) and splits the mesh into parts. It reads
`models/revolver/revolver.txt`, `models/fal/fal.txt` and `models/l42/l42.txt`, so build the guns
first. fp_rifle holds the FAL: the magazine change, finishing with a round chambered or charging
from empty, aiming. fp_sniper holds the L42A1: the right hand working the bolt after each shot,
the reload (bolt open, magazine changed, bolt closed), aiming. To regenerate after editing the script (Blender 4.x):

    blender --background --disable-autoexec Citizen.blend --python tools/export_character.py -- <Citizen textures dir> assets/models/character
